# AI compliance records

- `forms/`: official AI-02 and AI-03 form templates supplied for the course.
- `AI-02_AI_Audit_Report_Working.docx`: working AI-02 copy updated only for qualifying submission artifacts; keep the official form under `forms/` unchanged.
- `AI-03_AI_Disclosure_Form_Working.docx`: completed disclosure copy containing the verified course and student identity fields; the personal signature field is intentionally blank.
- `prompt_log.md`: chronological log of user prompts and final AI responses.
- `raw/`: exported raw transcripts or other original AI outputs when available.

The Markdown/PDF versions of the AI Audit Report, AI Disclosure and AI Critique
are stored directly in this directory. The original course forms remain unchanged
under `forms/`.
